# Customer Churn Prediction

## Project Overview

Customer retention is one of the biggest challenges faced by subscription-based businesses. In this project, I develop an end-to-end machine learning pipeline to predict customer churn using customer demographic, account, and service information. Multiple classification models will be developed and compared to identify the approach that best balances predictive performance with business value. The project follows a complete machine learning workflow, including data exploration, preprocessing, feature engineering, model evaluation, and business recommendations.

---

## Business Problem
Customer churn refers to the loss of customers over a given period of time. For subscription-based businesses, losing customers directly affects revenue and long-term growth. Since customer retention campaigns require time and financial resources, companies must identify which customers are most likely to leave so they can allocate their resources effectively.

Predicting customer churn enables organizations to make proactive, data-driven decisions instead of reacting after customers have already left. By identifying high-risk customers early, businesses can improve customer retention and reduce potential 
revenue loss.

---

## Project Objectives
The main objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze customer characteristics and identify factors associated with customer churn.
- Develop and compare multiple machine learning classification models for churn prediction.
- Evaluate model performance using appropriate classification metrics.
- Identify the most influential factors contributing to customer churn.
- Provide data-driven business recommendations to support customer retention strategies.
